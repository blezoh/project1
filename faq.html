Trademark questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Ask us.

What is a trademark?

A trademark is a name, logo, slogan or other sign that identifies your goods or services and sets them apart from others. Registering it gives you exclusive legal rights to use it for those goods and services.

Where can I register?

We currently file in Kenya (through KIPI) and the United States (through the USPTO). We plan to add Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and regional ARIPO filings.

How long does registration take?

It depends on the registry and whether your mark is objected to or opposed. In Kenya, accepted marks are advertised for a 60-day opposition period. In the US, recent USPTO data shows several months to a first review and roughly a year overall. We'll give you a realistic estimate for your case.

How long does a registration last?

A Kenyan registration lasts ten years from filing and can be renewed. US registrations require maintenance filings between the 5th and 6th year and renewal every 10 years. We track all your deadlines.

Should I register my name, my logo, or both?

A word mark protects the name in any style. A logo registration protects that specific design. If your budget allows only one, the word mark is usually the broadest. We'll advise based on your plans.

Do I need a local agent or lawyer?

Foreign applicants in Kenya must file through an appointed agent, and applicants living outside the US must use a US-licensed attorney for USPTO filings. We handle both for you.

What are "classes"?

Goods and services are grouped into 45 international classes (the Nice Classification). You pay per class you register in, for example clothing is class 25 and restaurants are class 43.

What if a similar mark already exists?

That's what the search is for. If something similar is on the register, we'll explain the risk and suggest options such as changing the name, narrowing the goods, or filing anyway with eyes open.

Can I use ™ and ®?

You can use ™ with any mark you claim as your own. The ® symbol is only for marks that are actually registered in the country where it's used.

Will this help with Amazon Brand Registry?

Amazon Brand Registry generally requires a pending or registered trademark in a supported country, including the US. A US filing is often the first step. Check Amazon's current requirements.

How do I pay?

You can pay in Kenya shillings or US dollars. We plan to accept M-Pesa, bank transfer and card (set up your payment providers before launch).
