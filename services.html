The Kenya process

  1. Search the register for similar marks
  2. File the application with KIPI
  3. Examination by the Registrar
  4. Advertisement in the Industrial Property Journal (60-day opposition period)
  5. Registration and certificate

Official steps and fees can change. We confirm current requirements for you.

The US process

  1. Clearance search of the USPTO database
  2. Application filed online by a US-licensed attorney
  3. Examining attorney review (office action if issues)
  4. Publication for opposition
  5. Registration certificate

Overseas applicants must be represented by a US-licensed attorney. Government fees are charged per class.
