Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How long does a Kenyan trademark take?

Timing varies with the registry's workload and whether anyone opposes the mark. Accepted marks are advertised for a 60-day opposition window before registration. We'll give you a realistic estimate in your free assessment.

Can a foreign company register in Kenya?

Yes, but foreign applicants must file through a locally appointed agent. We act as your agent and handle the required forms.

Do I need a US lawyer to file in the US?

If you live outside the United States, USPTO rules require you to be represented by a US-licensed attorney. We file through our US attorney partner.

How long does a registration last?

In Kenya a registered trademark lasts ten years and can be renewed. We track your dates and remind you.
