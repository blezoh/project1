AlamaTM, with its address at Nakuru, Kenya 98-20106, is committed to protecting your personal data. We refer to ourselves as the Company or we.

This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it when you use our services (the Services) or visit our website. It applies to everyone, wherever you live. We follow the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya and its regulations. We also respect the privacy rights of users in the United States, including California.

Information We Collect

We collect information you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, billing details, and messages you send us.

We also collect some information automatically through our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you visit.

We only collect what we need for the purposes below.

Why We Process Your Data

We process your personal data only when the law allows it. The reasons we rely on are:

  1. Your consent, for example for marketing messages and cookies that are not necessary.
  2. A contract, so we can provide the Services you asked for.
  3. A legal duty that applies to us.
  4. Protecting your vital interests or the interests of another person.
  5. Our legitimate interests, where these do not override your rights.

We use your data to provide and improve our Services, give you access to your account, process payments, analyse visits to our website, protect our business and customers, and, with your consent, send you marketing.

We do not combine data collected for different purposes. We keep your data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as the law requires.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tools to analyse how you use our website. These include:

  1. Necessary cookies that let you access the website.
  2. Functional cookies that help the website work properly.
  3. Analytics cookies that help us improve our content. They do not identify you personally until you enter your details in a form.
  4. Advertising cookies that help show you more relevant ads.

We ask for your consent before we use cookies that are not necessary. You can refuse or withdraw consent at any time through your browser or our cookie banner. If you refuse, some features of the website may not work properly. Where our website receives a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your information.

Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with service providers who help us run the Services, such as payment processors, hosting providers, and email tools. They may only use your data on our instructions. We may also share data when the law requires it. If we ever share data for targeted advertising, you can opt out as described below.

Email Marketing

We send marketing emails in line with Kenyan law and the US CAN SPAM Act. Every marketing email has a clear way to unsubscribe, and we will honour your request within 10 business days.

Transfers Outside Your Country

We operate from Kenya, and some of our service providers store or process data in other countries. If you use our Services from outside Kenya, including from the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in Kenya and other countries. We only transfer personal data abroad where the law allows it and where there are proper safeguards or you have given consent.

Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, and alteration. These include firewalls, encryption, and secure servers. We review our security practices regularly.

If a data breach is likely to risk your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires. This includes the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya and the relevant state authorities in the United States.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  1. Be informed about how we use your data.
  2. Access your personal data.
  3. Correct false, misleading, or inaccurate data.
  4. Delete your personal data.
  5. Object to the processing of your data.
  6. Receive your data in a usable format and have it transferred to another controller.
  7. Withdraw your consent at any time.
  8. Opt out of the sale or sharing of your data and of targeted advertising.
  9. Not be treated unfairly for using your rights.

If you are in Kenya, these rights come from the Data Protection Act, 2019. If you live in the United States, for example in California or another state with a privacy law, they come from your state law. In California this means the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act. We did not sell personal data for money in the past 12 months.

To use any of these rights, email us at jessekimani674@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity first. We will respond within the time the law requires, and within 45 days for US requests. You may use an authorised agent to make a request for you.

Complaints

If you believe we have misused your data, please contact us first. If you are in Kenya, you also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at www.odpc.go.ke. If you are in the United States, you may contact your state attorney general or consumer protection office.

Children

Our Services are not meant for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect a child's data without the consent of a parent or guardian, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy
